# Type and Alignment

- Text sizes, each with one job: 26 page titles and the logo title (issue title, about title, `.heti h1`), 24 `.heti h2`, 20 `.heti h3` and an entry's bold lead, 18 the phone card title, 17 the reading column (`.heti` body), 16 (`--font-size-base`) the sidebar issue list, header links, the desktop card title and quotes, 14 (`--font-size-small`) dates, summaries, captions and buttons. Settled on 2026-10-29 from rendered before/after pages after the site had drifted to ten sizes (32 beside the 26 logo read as two systems); on 2026-11-04 the reading column moved to the rhythm MiaoYan's preview, its iPhone reader and tw93.fun share (maintainer): 17px body at 1.64 and 0.02em, headings at line height 1.4 with 1.4em above and 0.4em below an `h2` (1.3em above an `h3`) and the next block's top margin dropped, 1.15em between paragraphs, lists, quotes, tables and code blocks, monospace code blocks at 0.86em and 1.6, quotes beside a 3px warm bar, then on 2026-11-08 at 16px with the bar as tall as the text and 9px off it (maintainer: a one-line quote's 12.6px bar 16px away read short and far, at 17px its text read big). `--font-size-base` stays 16 because Tailwind's rem sizes and spacing follow it; the 17 is set on `.heti`. New text takes one of these; a new size needs a stated reason. Left outside on purpose: `sup` 12, the search clear button 12, and the newsletter email input 16, below which iOS zooms the page.
- Styles nothing can produce are deleted, not kept for later: on 2026-10-29 a selector audit against all 1152 built pages and every script removed about 220 selectors (Heti's modifier and helper classes, highlight.js, mermaid, plantuml, markmap, MathJax and emoji rules, the old `.header-link` / `.sr-only` docs styles, lightense leftovers). Before deleting a selector its class or attribute is missing from, also search the scripts: reader mode sets `data-reader-mode` through `dataset.readerMode`, and PhotoSwipe builds `pswp__button--close`, `pswp__counter` and `pswp__preloader` from a name at runtime, so neither appears literally. `.heti` rules for elements Markdown can write (`h4` to `h6`, `sup`, `sub`, `figure`, `abbr`, `cite`, `ins`) stay even when no issue uses them yet. Prove a deletion with computed styles: dump every element's computed style on the home page, an issue, about and both English twins at 393, 1200 and 1440 in both themes, before and after, and expect no difference.
- The logo is the same in `Header.astro` and `LeftSidebar.astro` (40px cup, 26px title 9px off it), so going from the home page to an issue the cup does not move; change both together. The whole row sits on one centre line, 46px down on every page and width, within 0.5px by ink at 1x, 2x and 3x: cup, title, links, round buttons and their icons. `header` and `.sidebar-header` get there with 4px of bottom padding, `#grid-main` and the title's top margin on an issue page, with no negative margin pulling one element against another. The cup and the round button are the same height, so their top and bottom edges line up. Latin capitals sit 1.3px lower in a line box than Han glyphs, so `:lang(en)` lifts them 1px. The 2026-10-30 rule that put the title 2px under the cup's centre is dropped (maintainer 2026-11-08: on a phone the cup read high and the title low); `tests/browser-regressions.test.js` fails on the compensating margins. The header's Blog, GitHub, RSS and search come in at the Tailwind `nav` screen (540px), not `md`, so Android tablets in portrait (600 to 767px, OPPO Pad Mini) get them; tw93.fun's `$nav-full` is the same 540px, change the two together (2026-10-31). Breakpoints written as `max-width` use 799.98px, since `max-width: 800px` and `min-width: 800px` both matched at exactly 800 and that width got the phone padding. Sidebar issue rows are 6px above and below, about a 38px pitch (tightened from 8px on 2026-10-31, maintainer: the rows read too loose); the desktop `.nav-groups` top margin cancels that padding, so change the two together.
- Audit sizes from what renders, not from the CSS: walk every visible text node on the home page, an issue, about and an English issue at 393 and 1440 and list size, weight and container. Then fold, inject the candidate on the live pages, and compare before/after captures.
- Align by ink, not boxes. Capture each element alone (hide the rest with `visibility`) and compare the vertical centres of the inked rows across 393 to 1920 in both languages; measure English links on words without descenders, since the `y` of "Weekly" pulls its centre about 3px low. Two things corrupt the numbers: a browser page zoom other than 100% (ego was at 110%, so a 1024px emulation laid out at 931 and captures no longer matched DOM coordinates; emulating with `mobile: true` at any width ignores that zoom), and the service worker, which also caches `astro preview` on localhost, so unregister it and clear its caches before measuring a local build.
- Signed-in comment states cannot be styled from here: the giscus iframe is cross-origin, and a `session` from its URL must never be reused. Rebuild that markup from giscus `components/CommentBox.tsx` inside the standalone widget page, inject the generated theme, and measure it there; check the live signed-in page by eye afterwards.
