# Type and Alignment

- Text sizes, each with one job: 26 page titles and the logo title (issue title, about title, `.heti h1`), 24 `.heti h2`, 20 `.heti h3` and an entry's bold lead, 18 the phone card title, 16 (`--font-size-base`) the reading column (`.heti` body, 17 until 2026-11-17), the sidebar issue list, header links, the desktop card title and quotes, 14 (`--font-size-small`) dates, summaries, captions and buttons. Settled on 2026-10-29 from rendered before/after pages after the site had drifted to ten sizes (32 beside the 26 logo read as two systems); on 2026-11-04 the reading column moved to the rhythm MiaoYan's preview, its iPhone reader and tw93.fun share (maintainer): 17px body at 1.64 and 0.02em, headings at line height 1.4 with 1.4em above and 0.4em below an `h2` (1.3em above an `h3`) and the next block's top margin dropped, 1.15em between paragraphs, lists, quotes, tables and code blocks, monospace code blocks at 0.86em and 1.6, quotes beside a 3px warm bar, then on 2026-11-08 at 16px with the bar as tall as the text and 9px off it (maintainer: a one-line quote's 12.6px bar 16px away read short and far, at 17px its text read big). `--font-size-base` stays 16 because Tailwind's rem sizes and spacing follow it. New text takes one of these; a new size needs a stated reason. Left outside on purpose: `sup` 12, the search clear button 12, and the newsletter email input 16, below which iOS zooms the page.
- Styles nothing can produce are deleted, not kept for later: on 2026-10-29 a selector audit against all 1152 built pages and every script removed about 220 selectors (Heti's modifier and helper classes, highlight.js, mermaid, plantuml, markmap, MathJax and emoji rules, the old `.header-link` / `.sr-only` docs styles, lightense leftovers). Before deleting a selector its class or attribute is missing from, also search the scripts: reader mode sets `data-reader-mode` through `dataset.readerMode`, and PhotoSwipe builds `pswp__button--close`, `pswp__counter` and `pswp__preloader` from a name at runtime, so neither appears literally. `.heti` rules for elements Markdown can write (`h4` to `h6`, `sup`, `sub`, `figure`, `abbr`, `cite`, `ins`) stay even when no issue uses them yet. Prove a deletion with computed styles: dump every element's computed style on the home page, an issue, about and both English twins at 393, 1200 and 1440 in both themes, before and after, and expect no difference.
- The logo is the same in `Header.astro` and `LeftSidebar.astro` (40px cup, 26px title 9px off it), so going from the home page to an issue the cup does not move; change both together. The whole row sits on one centre line, 46px down on every page and width, within 0.5px by ink at 1x, 2x and 3x: cup, title, links, round buttons and their icons. `header` and `.sidebar-header` get there with 4px of bottom padding, `#grid-main` and the title's top margin on an issue page, with no negative margin pulling one element against another. The cup and the round button are the same height, so their top and bottom edges line up. Latin capitals sit 1.3px lower in a line box than Han glyphs, so `:lang(en)` lifts them 1px. The 2026-10-30 rule that put the title 2px under the cup's centre is dropped (maintainer 2026-11-08: on a phone the cup read high and the title low); `tests/browser-regressions.test.js` fails on the compensating margins. The cup's ink sits 1px above the title's centre (maintainer 2026-11-19, picked B from rendered 0, 1 and 2px lifts at 393 and 1440: its pale lower half hung 5px under the title's ink and read low; 2px read high on a phone again), done as one `transform: translateY(-1px)` on `.logo svg, .content-logo svg` in `markdown.css`, so no box in the row moves. The header's Blog, GitHub, RSS and search come in at the Tailwind `nav` screen (540px), not `md`, so Android tablets in portrait (600 to 767px, OPPO Pad Mini) get them; tw93.fun's `$nav-full` is the same 540px, change the two together (2026-10-31). Breakpoints written as `max-width` use 799.98px, since `max-width: 800px` and `min-width: 800px` both matched at exactly 800 and that width got the phone padding. Sidebar issue rows are 6px above and below, about a 38px pitch (tightened from 8px on 2026-10-31, maintainer: the rows read too loose); the desktop `.nav-groups` top margin (-20px) cancels that padding and lifts the list 14px more, so its first issue stays level with an issue's first block; change it with the row padding and with the issue title's desktop bottom margin (20px, `--space-5`, since 2026-11-17, maintainer: 34px read as a band of empty space under the header row).
- Issue rhythm (maintainer 2026-11-17, after a desktop review found nine entries running together): an entry, the paragraph that opens with a bold title and a line break, stands 32px (`--space-8`) from the block before it, up from the 20px between plain paragraphs, and stays 10px under a section title; an `h2` that follows an entry or a photo row takes 2em (48px) above so a section still starts further off than an entry, while the about page and prose keep 1.4em. The address under an entry's title keeps the body size (14px shipped for an hour and the maintainer turned it down) in `--theme-link-quiet` (the cup's brown darkened to `#8c6239`, 5.4:1; `#d3a473` in dark), `word-break: break-all` so a long one fills the line; links inside prose keep `#1d9bf0`. A table holding pictures is a photo row with no grid lines, 8px between pictures and its outer edges on the column; all 47 tables in the 290 issues are photo rows. The cover caption and quotes share `--theme-text-muted`, and image placeholders are warm `#f2f0eb`. The reading column's width and the page's centre were left alone on purpose: narrowing it would break the logo and card alignment with the home page.
- Ground, faces and body size (maintainer 2026-11-17): the reading column is 16px, `--font-size-base`, down from the 17 of 2026-11-04 (maintainer: 17 read big; asked twice, after a rendered 16 against 17), on phones too, so the address line, lists and quotes are all 16 and the 17 rung is gone. The ground stays white `#fff` and `#21262b` in dark, as on tw93.fun: the product sites' warm paper `#fbfaf6` with a warm black `#1e1d1b` and warm Tailwind greys shipped for a few minutes (6052466) and the maintainer found the page worse for it, so do not propose a warm ground again. English reading text is Charter with no tracking (Georgia on Windows, Noto Serif on Android, Han still JinKai), since JinKai's Latin read as handwriting over a paragraph; the issue title, buttons and footer stay in JinKai with the logo and sidebar. Turned down after rendering: JinKai W05 as a real bold (barely heavier than the W04 the site already serves as regular, so entry titles stopped reading as bold; the synthesized bold stays).
- Links and the language button (maintainer 2026-11-17, both picked from rendered candidates): links in running text are `--theme-link`, mole.fit's link ink `#34558a` (7.5:1 on white) and `#8fb1de` in dark (6.9:1), replacing `#1d9bf0`, which read bright and cold at 3.0:1; tw93.fun's `$link-blue` is the same value, change the two together. The address under an entry title keeps its brown. The language button shows the language it switches to, `EN` on a Chinese page and `中` on an English one, as `.lang-label` (14px bold) in the same 36px round button in `HeaderRight.astro` and `post.astro`; the globe icon did not read as a language switch and is deleted. A pill showing both (`中 / EN`) was rendered and turned down as more than the job needs. No redirect by browser language: many readers in China run an English system, crawlers render in English, and the English issues are machine translations of the Chinese original; hreflang carries the pairing.
- Hairlines and tracking (maintainer 2026-11-17, after an inventory of every rendered border and letter-spacing on both sites): one hairline, `--theme-divider` `#eeeeee` (`#343a40` in dark), for the `hr` in an issue, the footer rule, table borders, the newsletter field and the comments (`line` in `generate-giscus-themes.js`, light and blog themes); before, the page drew `#e6e6e6`, tables `#ccc` and `#e5e7eb`, comments `#ebebeb` and dark `#555` beside `#343a40` and `#404040`. tw93.fun's `$line` is the same value, change them together, and a blog theme change needs its `?v=`. Tracking is 0.02em on `body`, so chrome matches the reading column; the fixed 0.5px it replaces was 0.036em on 14px summaries and dates and 0.019em on the 26px logo. English pages set `body` to 0, chrome included. A `<video>` inside an entry paragraph takes the image's 8px top margin: it sat 3.2px under its title where an image sits 11.
- Audit sizes from what renders, not from the CSS: walk every visible text node on the home page, an issue, about and an English issue at 393 and 1440 and list size, weight and container. Then fold, inject the candidate on the live pages, and compare before/after captures.
- Align by ink, not boxes. Capture each element alone (hide the rest with `visibility`) and compare the vertical centres of the inked rows across 393 to 1920 in both languages; measure English links on words without descenders, since the `y` of "Weekly" pulls its centre about 3px low. Two things corrupt the numbers: a browser page zoom other than 100% (ego was at 110%, so a 1024px emulation laid out at 931 and captures no longer matched DOM coordinates; emulating with `mobile: true` at any width ignores that zoom), and the service worker, which also caches `astro preview` on localhost, so unregister it and clear its caches before measuring a local build.
- Signed-in comment states cannot be styled from here: the giscus iframe is cross-origin, and a `session` from its URL must never be reused. Rebuild that markup from giscus `components/CommentBox.tsx` inside the standalone widget page, inject the generated theme, and measure it there; check the live signed-in page by eye afterwards.
