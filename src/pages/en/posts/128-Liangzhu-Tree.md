## Trending Tools

**Experiencing ChatGPT Plugins**  
<https://openai.com/waitlist/plugins>  
💡 The experience genuinely shocked me. Imagine when it matures: maybe the way users operate software becomes one big input box, and interaction design, user design and frontend work all get swept away by an input box plus a protocol for displaying results, a return to the essence of meeting user needs in the simplest, fastest and best way. Its strong context handling may even make it more convenient than a pure lookup tool.  
😂 By then the software industry should see some interesting changes: app development would have no frontend or interaction work as we know it, you could even hand data to AI and have it generate the API for you, and data itself would become more important.  
🏂 While trying plugins, I found dozens of them available, covering tools, travel, daily life, games, learning and more, though most are built abroad, and you can also develop, customize and publish your own following the manifest. You can use at most 3 plugins at a time, it is currently a bit slower than GPT-4, and when it cannot find the data it tries another way to look it up.  
🤖 I asked Expedia to plan a trip to Tokyo, and it automatically arranged flights, hotels, sightseeing and car rental, even showing product cards that carry the parameters to the official site for one-click booking. It also supports interaction and remembers context, much more convenient than searching yourself.  
🔮 I also played a text game, a kind of life-sim where you choose what to do step by step and it calculates your growth stats, good for anyone who likes raising-type games. There is room for imagination here too, like the Telegram bot Samantha I recommended last time.  
🧐 The engineer's role is slowly losing its fixed job boundaries and becoming about solving problems, and the ability to build a product independently matters more. This wave of AI will gradually flatten what makes each role special, as if one person plus an AI co-pilot becomes a whole team.
